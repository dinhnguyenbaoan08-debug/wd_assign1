This is a normal and simple one-day schedule for a student in Swinburne University. Future students can use this study plan as an example for them to register and manage their time effectively when they start their studies at Swinburne University of Technology.

One-Day Study Plan
Time Activity Location Good Tips
8:30am-10:30am Tutorial Class 1 ATC building Arrive 10-15 minutes early before the class starts.
10:30am-12:30pm Tutorial Class 2 EN Building Before doing second class, some class will finish a bit early so you can go and wrap some energy drink to wake you up.
After 12:30pm Lunch Break/ Free Time Library/ Eating Street Take a break and have lunch with friends. You can also use this time to do some assignments or study.
