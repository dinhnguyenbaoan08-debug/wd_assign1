Practical Tips

Here are some practical tips for future students starting at Swinburne:

  • Studying:Use the library early in the morning and after 5pm- It is quiet and spacious.
  • Transportation:Swinburne University have the station called Glenferrie- It is right on the school campus so it is very convenient.
  • Social Activities:Social activities can be registered for through school clubs, such as swimming, badminton, and dancing- these activities are very helpful for physical training.
These are my recommendations for future students starting at Swinburne University. I hope they will find them useful and helpful in their journey.
